Step 5 of 6 — At 16: the second gate

The colleges themselves

Brighton is an 11-16 town. Most state secondaries end at 16; post-16 runs through two colleges — and they are genuinely good, which is why so many pathways converge there.

BHASVICOFSTED OUTSTANDING
Outstanding in all areas (Dec 2022) — a decade continuously Outstanding — and on recent results the top-performing non-selective sixth form college in England. This is the free option most roads lead to; it would be a destination school in any city.
Varndean CollegeOFSTED GOOD
Good (Mar 2024) with Outstanding for behaviour, personal development and high-needs provision. 2025 A-levels: ~50% A*–B, ~98% pass. The area's only International Baccalaureate provider (avg score ~34) — a genuine differentiator, and typically the broader entry profile of the two.
